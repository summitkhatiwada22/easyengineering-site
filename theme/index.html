About Us

Opening roads through Nepal's toughest terrain

Established in 2017 and registered with the Government of Nepal's Office of the Company Registrar, Easy Engineering and Construction Pvt. Ltd. (EEC) builds access roads through Nepal's most remote and technically demanding mountain terrain — hard-rock excavation, controlled blasting, and construction along steep cliff faces in areas with little to no existing infrastructure.

Mission & Vision

To construct safe, durable access roads through Nepal's most challenging mountain terrain — enabling hydropower development and connecting remote communities to opportunity.

To be Nepal's most trusted name in mountain infrastructure, recognized for technical expertise, safety, and the ability to build where others cannot.
